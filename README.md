# AIready (aiready.nz)

AIready (aiready.nz, spelled A-I-ready) is an Auckland-based AEO, GEO and agentic commerce audit service for New Zealand businesses. We measure and improve how ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity find, understand and recommend a business.

## In the news

On 29 October 2026, TechDay NZ covered AIready's benchmark of how readable NZ business websites are to AI crawlers. Same story, four titles, by Joseph Gabriel Lagonsin, News Editor:

- [IT Brief New Zealand: AIready audit finds many NZ sites hard for crawlers](https://itbrief.co.nz/story/aiready-audit-finds-many-nz-sites-hard-for-crawlers)
- [ChannelLife New Zealand: AIready audit finds many NZ sites hard for crawlers](https://channellife.co.nz/story/aiready-audit-finds-many-nz-sites-hard-for-crawlers)
- [eCommerceNews New Zealand: AIready audit finds many NZ sites hard for crawlers](https://ecommercenews.co.nz/story/aiready-audit-finds-many-nz-sites-hard-for-crawlers)
- [SecurityBrief New Zealand: AIready audit finds many NZ sites hard for crawlers](https://securitybrief.co.nz/story/aiready-audit-finds-many-nz-sites-hard-for-crawlers)

The coverage reported a 158-audit snapshot (scans August to October 2026): median 55/100, 28% scoring 70 or above, 51% scoring 40 to 69 and 21% (33 sites) below 40. It named three common blockers: crawler access restrictions (WAFs and bot rules), client-side JavaScript rendering gaps, and missing Schema.org JSON-LD. The live benchmark has since grown to 355 sites; see [aiready.nz/benchmark](https://aiready.nz/benchmark) for the latest figures. A readiness score measures how easily a scanner can access and understand a site; it does not guarantee AI citations.
